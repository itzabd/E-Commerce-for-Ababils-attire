# Quick Start: How to Process a New Order

1. **Log in** to the Admin Dashboard.
2. Go to the **Orders** section in the sidebar.
3. Look for orders with the status **"Pending"**.
4. Click on the order to open its details.
5. Check the **bKash Payment Details** submitted by the customer (TrxID, Last 4 digits, Amount).
6. Open your bKash app or check your SMS to **verify** that you actually received this payment.
7. Once verified, change the Order Status from "Pending" to **"Processing"**.
8. Prepare the product (bake the cake / tailor the dress).
9. When handing over to delivery, update the status to **"Shipped"**.

# Quick Start: How to Add a Product

1. **Log in** to the Admin Dashboard.
2. Go to the **Products** section.
3. Click the **"Add Product"** button.
4. Enter the **Title** and **Description**.
5. Select the **Category** (Dress or Cake).
6. Set the **Price**.
7. Upload high-quality **Images**.
8. Click **"Save Product"**. It is now live on the website.

# Quick Start: How to Reconcile a bKash Advance

Because the store doesn't connect directly to bKash's system, you must do this manually:
1. When a new order arrives, find the customer's provided **TrxID** in the Order Details.
2. Open your personal/merchant bKash statement.
3. Search for the exact **TrxID**.
4. Confirm the amount received matches the required advance.
5. If it matches, process the order. If it doesn't, contact the customer using their provided phone number.
