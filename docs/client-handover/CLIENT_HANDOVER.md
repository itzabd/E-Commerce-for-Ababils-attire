# Client Handover Document
## Ababil's Attire by Sanjida Bethi

**Welcome to your new e-commerce store!**
This folder contains all the documentation you need to run, manage, and understand your website.

### What Has Been Delivered
1. **A Customer Storefront:** A beautiful, responsive website where customers can browse dresses and cakes, add them to a shopping bag, and place orders.
2. **An Admin Dashboard:** A secure, private area where you can add new products, manage customer orders, track payments, and update store settings.
3. **Database & Hosting Integration:** The site is connected to a Supabase backend to safely store your products, orders, and images.

### How to Use It
Start by reading the included manuals. You do not need to be a technical expert to run the store.
- **For You (The Admin):** Read the `ADMIN_MANUAL.md` to learn how to log in, add products, and manage orders.
- **For Quick Tasks:** Check `QUICK_START_ADMIN.md` for fast, step-by-step guides on everyday tasks.
- **For Your Customers:** You can refer to `CUSTOMER_MANUAL.md` if a customer asks how to use the site, or adapt it for a "How to Order" post on your Facebook page.

### Important URLs / Routes
- **Customer Storefront:** `https://your-domain.com/` (or your staging link)
- **Admin Login:** `https://your-domain.com/admin`
- **Track Order Page:** `https://your-domain.com/track-order`

*(Note: Replace `https://your-domain.com` with your actual live website address once launched.)*

### Admin Access
To log into the Admin Dashboard, use the email and password provided to you by your developer. Keep this password safe. Do not share it with customers.

### Basic Maintenance
The website is designed to be self-sufficient. Your primary maintenance will be:
- Adding/removing products as your inventory changes.
- Updating order statuses so customers are kept informed.
- Checking your bKash app to verify payments manually.

### Known Limitations
- **Manual bKash Verification:** The system does not automatically deduct money or verify bKash transactions. Customers submit their Transaction ID (TrxID) and you must verify it on your phone before processing the order. This saves you from expensive merchant API fees and complex setups.

### Technical Support
If you encounter technical errors, need to reset your password, or want to add new features in the future, please contact your developer. For deeper technical information, developers can refer to `SYSTEM_MAINTENANCE.md`.
