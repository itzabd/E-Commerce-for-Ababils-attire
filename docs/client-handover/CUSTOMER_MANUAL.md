# Customer User Manual

Welcome to Ababil's Attire by Sanjida Bethi! This guide explains how to browse our store, place orders, and track your items.

## 1. Browsing Products
- **Dresses & Cakes:** Use the navigation menu at the top of the page to browse our categories.
- **Product Details:** Click on any product to see more images, read the description, and view the price.
- **Options:** For dresses, you might need to select a size. For cakes, you might select a flavor or weight.

## 2. Adding to My Bag
- Once you've selected your options, click the **"Add to Bag"** button.
- You can view your bag at any time by clicking the shopping bag icon in the top right corner.
- In your bag, you can review your items, change quantities, or remove items before proceeding to checkout.

## 3. Checkout Process
When you are ready to buy, click **"Proceed to Checkout"** from your bag.
1. **Delivery Information:** Enter your full name, phone number, and complete delivery address.
2. **Review Order:** Check your items and the total cost (including any delivery fees).
3. **Payment Method:** We currently accept payments via **bKash** (Advance + Cash on Delivery for the rest).

## 4. Making a Payment (bKash)
To confirm your order, you need to send an advance payment via your bKash app.
- The checkout page will display our bKash number and the required advance amount.
- **Step 1:** Open your bKash app and "Send Money" or "Payment" to the provided number.
- **Step 2:** Note down the **Transaction ID (TrxID)** from the bKash confirmation.
- **Step 3:** On the checkout page, enter your **TrxID**, the **Last 4 digits** of the bKash number you sent money from, and a **Reference Name**.
- **Step 4:** Click **"Place Order"**.

## 5. Order Confirmation
- After placing your order, you will see a confirmation screen with your **Order ID**.
- **Please save this Order ID**, as you will need it to track your order.

## 6. Tracking Your Order
- Click on the **"Track Order"** link in the navigation menu.
- Enter your **Phone Number** and your **Order ID**.
- You will see the current status of your order (e.g., Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered).

## 7. Contact & Support
- If you have any questions, need to change your order, or need help with a payment, please visit our **Contact Page**.
- You can reach out to us via phone or our social media links provided on the site.
