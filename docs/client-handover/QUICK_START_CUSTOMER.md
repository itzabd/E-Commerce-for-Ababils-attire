# Quick Start: How to Place an Order

1. Browse the site and find a Dress or Cake you like.
2. Select your options (like size or flavor) and click **"Add to Bag"**.
3. Open your shopping bag (top right corner) and click **"Proceed to Checkout"**.
4. Fill in your Name, Phone, and Delivery Address.
5. Look at the payment instructions for bKash.
6. Send the requested advance amount from your bKash app to the store's number.
7. Enter the **TrxID**, **Last 4 Digits**, and **Reference Name** into the checkout form.
8. Click **"Place Order"** and save your Order ID!

# Quick Start: How to Track an Order

1. Go to the website and click **"Track Order"** in the top menu.
2. Enter the **Phone Number** you used during checkout.
3. Enter your **Order ID** (provided on the confirmation page).
4. Click **"Track"** to see if your order is Pending, Processing, Shipped, or Delivered.
